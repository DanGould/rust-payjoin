Why not one open mailbox for donations?

  • Any stranger could start a payjoin and see one of Bob's coins, every time, without ever paying
  • And keep a proof for a coin that stays unspent
  • Anyone could fill the mailbox
  • So a donation address stays a plain silent payment address
